# Roadmap Reports

Roadmap reports are advisory workflow records. They propose small next actions for the topic-centered mathematical study wiki.

## Files

- `reports/roadmap/next-actions.md`: current safest next task and alternatives.
- `data/research_queue.yml`: topic prioritization and safest-next-task registry.
- `data/topic_maturity.yml`: canonical topic maturity tracker.
- `data/review_backlog.yml`: actionable audit and review issue tracker.
- `data/notation.yml`: canonical notation registry for audits.
- `data/source_candidates.yml`: candidate source registry.
- `reports/reviews/`: dated audit reports.

## Rules

- Candidate sources are not citations and do not support claims until approved and ingested.
- Do not download, fetch, stage, or intake a new paper without explicit user approval.
- Prefer existing-source expansion before new-source intake when existing claims are enough.
- Keep actions small, reviewable, and concept-first.
- Include approval requirement, new-source requirement, and an exact prompt the user can paste.
- Do not turn roadmap reports into paper summaries.
- Do not create unsupported mathematical claims.

## Action Types

- `existing-source expansion`
- `new-source intake`
- `topic-page polishing`
- `graph cleanup`
- `notation normalization`
- `human-review task`
