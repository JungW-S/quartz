# Source Note Instructions

Source notes are concise provenance records for real sources. They are not long paper summaries.

## Rules

- Do not create a source note for a fake or hypothetical source.
- Record bibliographic facts, source locations, extracted-unit indexes, and review flags.
- Keep notes short enough to support navigation and verification.
- Do not copy long passages from sources.
- Mark uncertain extraction points with `<!-- NEEDS-HUMAN-REVIEW -->`.
- Do not add source-backed claims unless the source location is recorded.
- User discussion notes are not paper, book, or lecture sources. Keep them under `content/sources/discussions/` and structure them through `data/discussion_notes.yml`.
- Discussion-based material may guide exposition or notation decisions, but it must not support theorem-level claims or replace source-backed examples.
