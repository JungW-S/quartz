## Imported Claim Index

- `hl10-categories-cell-definition`: definition of the subcategories $\mathcal C_\ell$ by restrictions on roots of Drinfeld polynomials; source location: Section 3.8, Definition 3.1, printed pp.8-9.
- `hl10-cell-monoidal-grothendieck-ring`: $\mathcal C_\ell$ is abelian monoidal and its Grothendieck ring is a polynomial ring in the fundamental-module classes; source location: Section 3.8, Proposition 3.2, printed p.9.
- `hl10-c0-simple-object-description`: simple objects of $\mathcal C_0$ are tensor products of fundamental modules and tensor products of simple objects in $\mathcal C_0$ are simple; source location: Section 3.8, Example 3.3, printed p.9.
- `hl10-c1-monoidal-categorification-conjecture`: for $\ell=1$, the conjectural cluster-algebra comparison sends cluster variables and frozen variables to classes of distinguished simple objects and makes $\mathcal C_1$ a monoidal categorification; source location: Section 4.4, Conjecture 4.6, printed pp.12-13.
- `hl10-a3-c1-prime-simple-example`: compact type $A_3$ example for \(\mathcal C_1\), prime simple objects, and one exchange-relation tensor-product consequence; source location: Example 4.8, printed p.13.
