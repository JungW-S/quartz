## Intake Scope

This source note records the lower-level quiver-Hecke/KLR material imported for the `quiver-hecke-algebras` prerequisite page. It is not a paper summary.

## Imported Claim Index

- `brundan13-quiver-hecke-algebra-definition`: symmetric quiver-Hecke algebra $H_\alpha$ by generators and relations, source location: Section 2, arXiv pp.6-7.
- `brundan13-diagrammatic-quiver-hecke-category`: diagrammatic interpretation by colored strands, dots, crossings, vertical composition, and horizontal tensor product, source location: Section 2, arXiv pp.7-8.
- `brundan13-quiver-hecke-basis-theorem`: monomial basis theorem for $H_\alpha$, source location: Theorem 2.6, arXiv pp.8-9.
- `brundan13-khovanov-lauda-categorification-theorem`: Khovanov-Lauda isomorphism from Lusztig's integral form to the projective Grothendieck group, source location: Theorem 3.9, arXiv p.15.
