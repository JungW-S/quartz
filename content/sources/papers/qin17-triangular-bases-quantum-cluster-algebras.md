## Notation Notes

- Qin writes the seed-dependent degree lattice as \(D(t)\). Topic pages use this notation only when discussing the Qin-style cluster-algebra layer.
- Qin's source supplies pointed elements and dominance order. It does not by itself supply the full JP25 left/right localized-crystal convention.

## Not Imported
