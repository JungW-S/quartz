## Intake Scope

This note records only the ordinary PBW material needed before `Localized PBW Parametrizations`. It is not a book summary.

The imported scope is:

- Chapter 40.1-40.2 for braid-group root-vector factors and PBW-type bases of \(U^+(w,e)\);

- Chapter 42.1 only as optional finite simply-laced context for reduced-word changes.

## Imported Claim Index

- `lusztig94-pbw-positive-factors`: reduced-expression braid transforms of simple positive generators lie in \(U^+\), source location: Chapter 40, Proposition 40.1.3, p.320.
- `lusztig94-pbw-basis-uplus-w`: exponent-vector monomials attached to a reduced expression form a basis of \(U^+(w,e)\), source location: Chapter 40, Proposition 40.2.1, p.321.
- `lusztig94-integral-pbw-basis`: the same PBW-type construction has an integral \(A\)-basis version, source location: Chapter 41, Proposition 41.1.4, p.325.
- `lusztig94-canonical-basis-congruence`: each integral PBW-type element determines a unique canonical-basis element modulo the \(v^{-1}\)-part, source location: Chapter 41, Proposition 41.1.6, p.325.
